## Freeze implication

M30 reduced the specialist-thumbnail gap with bounded evidence rather than an overall score. The gap remains material for semantic composition and validated human readability. M31 reduced caption dependence without treating ASR as ground truth: machine transcript matches remain advisory and exact bounded user confirmation is artifact-bound. Speech evidence remains limited when a local model is unavailable, audio/language conditions are poor, a spoken URL must be normalized, or a whole-video absence claim would be required. General creative editing remains intentionally out of scope.

The scanner contracts, repair verifier, Release Contract trust policy, receipt canonicalization, RevisionMapper, and Judge Proof provenance are core-frozen after M29. Later work may consume or extend their public typed boundaries, but must not casually refactor or weaken them.
