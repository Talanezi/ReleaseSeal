## Freeze implication

M30 reduced the specialist-thumbnail gap with bounded evidence rather than an overall score. The gap remains material for semantic composition and validated human readability. Trustworthy transcript evidence for Release Contract checks remains a higher-risk alternative because it touches a more sensitive trust boundary.

The scanner contracts, repair verifier, Release Contract trust policy, receipt canonicalization, RevisionMapper, and Judge Proof provenance are core-frozen after M29. Later work may consume or extend their public typed boundaries, but must not casually refactor or weaken them.
